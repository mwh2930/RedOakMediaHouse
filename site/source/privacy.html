Draft for review · September 9, 2026

This page documents the website’s current features. It is not yet a finalized privacy policy. Company legal details, service-provider processing, information use and retention must be confirmed against the company’s legal records before public release.

What this page covers

This page concerns the Red Oak Media House company website and its project inquiry feature. It does not describe the privacy practices of individual applications or client websites.

Information you choose to send

The project inquiry form asks for your name, email address, project type and project description. Completing the form opens a draft in your email application addressed to Support@redoakmediahouse.com. You choose whether to send that email.

The website’s form does not submit these details to a website database or save them in browser storage. If you send the email, its contents are delivered through email services to the business inbox. Avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a project inquiry.

Interactive features

Diagram selections and motion controls use temporary page memory. The website’s custom page code does not store these preferences in cookies or browser storage, and they are not retained when the page reloads.

Hosting, email and technical information

Website hosting, access controls and email delivery involve service providers. Their processing can include technical information such as IP addresses, access records and message metadata. The applicable providers, information collected, purposes and retention periods are still being confirmed for this policy.

No analytics or advertising tracker has been added to the website’s custom page code. This does not establish what the hosting or access-control services collect.

Privacy questions

Contact Support@redoakmediahouse.com with a question about your information or a request to review, correct or delete information you have sent. Describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive details.

Details awaiting confirmation

The final policy must confirm the legal business identity, how inquiries are used and shared, retention practices, applicable privacy rights and request procedures, provider cookies and tracking behavior, and how policy changes are communicated. An effective date will be added when that review is complete.
