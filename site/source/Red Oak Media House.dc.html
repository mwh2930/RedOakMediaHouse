How we work

Four questions.
One clear path.

Each stage answers a different question. Together, they turn a loose idea into something people can understand and use.

  1. 01Define

    Who is it for?

    Understand the audience, the problem to solve, and what a useful outcome looks like.

  2. 02Design

    How should it work?

    Map the journey. Arrange the content and controls. Make the next step easy to understand.

  3. 03Build

    How do we make it?

    Develop the website or iOS application, connecting the design to working behavior.

  4. 04Refine

    What is ready to launch?

    Test the experience, resolve the details, and prepare the next steps for release.
